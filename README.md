# Elven Beauty Launch

Create a complete, production-ready, multi-page website for **Elven Beauty Hub** — a premium unisex beauty salon in Lagos. The website must be significantly better than https://www.brushesandclippers.com/ in design quality, visual hierarchy, animation, responsiveness, and conversion focus.

### BRAND DETAILS
- **Company Name**: Elven Beauty Hub
- **Taglines**: 
  - “Look Good • Feel Good • Be You”
  - “Your Beauty Our Passion”
  - “We create the look you deserve.”
- **Logo**: Gold silhouette of a woman’s head with flowing hair + text “ELVEN BEAUTY HUB” + tagline “BEAUTY • CONFIDENCE • YOU”. Use the logo as an SVG or high-quality image placeholder. Place it in the navbar and footer.
- **Brand Colors** (strictly use these):
  - Primary: Warm Beige (#F5F0E6)
  - Secondary: Cream (#FAF7F2)
  - Accent/Gold: #C9A227 or #D4AF37
  - Text: Deep Black (#1A1A1A) and soft charcoal
  - Dark sections: near-black (#0F0F0F)
- **Contact**:
  - Address: Shop 27, Nipost Shopping Complex, Sabo Yaba, Lagos
  - WhatsApp / Phone: 0803 175 9528 (make it clickable wa.me/2348031759528)
  - Instagram: @elvenbeautyhub
- **Opening highlight**: “Coming Soon — September 13”
- **Key selling points**: Unisex Salon • Professional Beauty Experts • Clean & Serene Environment • Affordable Prices • Free WiFi • Walk-ins Welcome | Appointments Preferred

### SERVICES (use these exactly)
- Hair Styling & Wig Installation
- Braiding & Protective Styles
- Barbering (Men & Boys)
- Kids Hair Services
- Nails (Manicure & Pedicure)
- Lashes & Brows
- Hair Treatment
- Sales of Beauty Products
- And More…

### STRUCTURE — EXACTLY 4 PAGES (separate HTML files)

1. **index.html** (Home)
2. **services.html**
3. **gallery.html**
4. **contact.html**

### DESIGN REQUIREMENTS (make it premium)
- Modern, elegant, luxurious beauty-salon aesthetic (think high-end African beauty brand)
- Soft cream/beige backgrounds with gold accents and black typography
- Smooth scroll, subtle fade-in animations (AOS or pure CSS), elegant hover effects
- Fully responsive (mobile-first)
- Sticky transparent navbar that becomes solid on scroll
- Prominent “Book via WhatsApp” floating button + sticky header CTA
- Beautiful hero sections with large typography and overlay images
- High-quality placeholder images (use unsplash or describe realistic beauty/salon images of Black models)
- Clean, modern sans-serif fonts (e.g. Inter + Playfair Display or similar elegant pairing)
- Soft shadows, rounded cards, generous white space
- Footer with logo, quick links, contact info, social icons, and “BEAUTY • CONFIDENCE • YOU”

### PAGE-BY-PAGE CONTENT

**1. index.html**
- Hero: Full-screen elegant image of a beautiful Black woman with perfect hair/makeup. Big headline “Your Beauty. Our Passion.” + subtext + two CTAs (“Book Appointment” → WhatsApp, “View Services”)
- Stats bar: 6,955+ Followers | Unisex Salon | Free WiFi | Coming Sept 13
- About teaser section
- Services overview (6–8 service cards with icons)
- “Why Choose Elven” section (Professional Experts, Clean Environment, Affordable, Satisfaction Guaranteed)
- Testimonials (3 elegant cards)
- Instagram teaser + CTA
- Final CTA banner before footer

**2. services.html**
- Hero with title “Our Services”
- Detailed service sections with beautiful cards or grid for each category:
  - Hair Styling & Wig Installation
  - Braiding & Protective Styles
  - Barbering (Men & Boys)
  - Kids Hair
  - Nails
  - Lashes & Brows
  - Hair Treatments
  - Beauty Products
- Each service has short description + “Book Now” WhatsApp button
- “Coming Soon September 13” banner

**3. gallery.html**
- Elegant masonry or grid gallery
- Categories filter (All, Hair, Braids, Nails, Lashes, Barbering, Kids)
- High-quality placeholder images of real beauty transformations
- Lightbox-ready structure
- CTA at bottom: “Want this look? Book via WhatsApp”

**4. contact.html**
- Hero “Visit Us” / “Book Your Appointment”
- Full address with Google Maps embed placeholder
- WhatsApp button (large & prominent)
- Phone number
- Instagram link
- Opening note: “Walk-ins Welcome | Appointments Preferred”
- Simple contact form (Name, Phone, Service interested in, Message) that can later be connected to WhatsApp or email
- “Free WiFi • Clean & Serene Environment” highlights

### TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- Pure HTML + CSS + minimal vanilla JS (or Tailwind CDN if preferred)
- All pages share the same navbar and footer
- Mobile menu (hamburger)
- Smooth scrolling
- SEO-friendly titles and meta descriptions
- Fast loading, clean code
- Use semantic HTML5
- All WhatsApp links must open chat with pre-filled message like: “Hi Elven Beauty Hub, I would like to book an appointment.”

Make the entire website feel luxurious, trustworthy, and distinctly African-luxury while remaining clean and modern. Make it clearly better than the Brushes & Clippers site in every aspect (typography, spacing, imagery, CTA hierarchy, mobile experience, and overall polish).

Output the complete code for all 4 pages separately, clearly labeled.
